# CiviTrack AI Product definition

## Audience
- **Citizens**: Residents who want a fast, reliable, and transparent way to report civic issues (potholes, garbage, lighting defects) directly to municipal authorities.
- **Municipal Officials / Inspectors**: Government employees managing resolution workflows, dispatching repair crews, and tracking analytics.

## Brand Identity
- **Voice**: Professional, authoritative, responsive, and modern.
- **Goal**: Make civic reporting feel high-tech, fast-paced, and highly functional, avoiding generic and clinical layouts.
